THE VISION BEHIND SHASTRY GROUP.

Keshav Shastry

Founder of Shastry Group.

Keshav Shastry is the founder of Shastry Group, a creative entrepreneur driven by a passion for technology, storytelling, entertainment and innovation.

With an interest in exploring different creative disciplines, he established Shastry Group with a vision of bringing multiple forms of creativity together under one organisation.

His vision is to build a company where ideas are not restricted to one industry or medium, but can be transformed into animations, music, books, comics, games and digital experiences.

He believes that every great creation begins with an idea, and the right combination of imagination, dedication and execution can turn that idea into something extraordinary.

“Great ideas should never remain just ideas. They deserve to become something real.”
Keshav Shastry
