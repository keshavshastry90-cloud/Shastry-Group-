03 · About Shastry Group

MORE THAN CREATION. A UNIFIED STUDIO.

Shastry Group was built on a clear conviction: great stories and bold ideas should never be confined to a single medium.

As an independent, founder-led creative studio and digital production house, we operate six interconnected creative divisions—allowing a single original universe to live simultaneously as a cinematic animation, an original soundtrack, a full-length novel, a graphic novel series, a playable browser game, and a flagship web platform.

We combine disciplined creative direction with modern engineering to turn ambitious concepts into finished, audience-ready experiences.

Our Core Philosophy

"Every great creation begins with an idea. We help transform that idea into reality."

07 · Founder & Leadership

THE VISION BEHIND SHASTRY GROUP.

Keshav Shastry

Founder & Creative Director, Shastry Group

Keshav Shastry is the founder of Shastry Group, a multidisciplinary creative entrepreneur, author, comic creator, and digital builder driven by a passion for storytelling, entertainment, and technology.

Having authored published children's and sci-fi books, scripted superhero and fantasy comic series, produced over 1,500 digital videos generating 1.1M+ views and 22,700+ watch hours, and built interactive web platforms, he founded Shastry Group to bring these creative disciplines together under one cohesive organisation.

Looking ahead, his vision is to build original intellectual properties and digital experiences that turn bold ideas into enduring creative worlds.
